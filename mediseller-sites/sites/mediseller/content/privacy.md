This policy explains how **[Registered company name]** ("MediSeller", "we") handles personal data collected through this website, our chat assistant, WhatsApp, email and phone. We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and apply GDPR-style principles to visitors from other countries.

## What we collect

- **Contact details** you give us: name, phone/WhatsApp number, email, country, city, company.
- **Order request details**: products, quantities, notes, preferred call time.
- **Prescriptions** you choose to upload. These are health data — stored in a private, access-controlled store and used only to process your order.
- **Chat messages** with our website assistant.
- **Technical data**: IP address, browser, pages visited, and — only if you accept cookies — analytics and advertising identifiers (Google Analytics, Meta Pixel).

## Why we use it

To respond to your request, prepare quotes, verify prescriptions for prescription-only medicines, arrange shipping, keep business records, prevent fraud, and — only with consent — measure and improve our marketing.

## Who we share it with

Service providers that run our systems (hosting: Cloudflare; database and file storage: Supabase; email: Resend; spreadsheet: Google; AI assistant: Google Gemini), couriers and logistics partners for shipping, and authorities where required by law. We do not sell personal data.

## AI assistant

Messages you send to the chat assistant are processed by an AI service (Google Gemini) to generate replies and are stored so our agents can follow up. Do not share sensitive information in chat that you do not want stored.
