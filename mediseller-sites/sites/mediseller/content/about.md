**MediSeller** was built for one problem: people around the world who cannot get the medicine they need — because it is not available in their country, costs far too much, or takes months to arrive.

India manufactures a large share of the world's medicines, including high-quality generics of many critical-care drugs. We connect patients, doctors, pharmacies, importers and resellers with that supply — and stay with every order until it arrives.

## What makes us different

- **A person, not just a website.** Every order is handled by an agent who confirms price, availability, documents and shipping with you.
- **Built for hard-to-reach markets.** We focus on Southeast Asia, Africa, Russia & CIS, Eastern Europe and other markets where critical medicines are hard to access.
- **Patients and businesses.** From a single patient's monthly supply to recurring export orders for distributors.
- **Responsible.** Prescription-only medicines go out only against a valid prescription, and we never give medical advice.

## Our promise

Clear quotes. Genuine products from licensed sources. Honest timelines. And a real person to talk to.
