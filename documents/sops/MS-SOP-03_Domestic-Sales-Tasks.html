Do these India-specific tasks in addition to the Common Tasks (MS-SOP-02). Domestic follow-ups start after Global follow-ups.

Daily checklist दैनिक जाँच सूची

  • D01 · B2 · B4
    Take 1 IndiaMART buy lead at block B2 and 1 more at block B4.
  • D02 · B3
    India buy leads (T-1).
  • D03 · B4
    Domestic follow-ups (after Global): On-Payment customers, Active customers, ExportersIndia + TradeIndia leads. Scope: India B2B + retail.
  • D04 · B5
    Last-week buy leads (dates 7, 8, 9 in the example), T-2.
  • D05 · B8
    IndiaMART calling block (3–6 PM for Shift 2).
  • D06 · 6–7 PM [confirm]
    Direct leads, 6–7 PM.
Block times: B1–B10 are counted from your shift start. See the day-structure table in MS-SOP-01 for the clock times of each shift.
ब्लॉक का समय आपकी शिफ्ट शुरू होने से गिना जाता है — विवरण MS-SOP-01 में।

T-1 = leads from yesterday · T-2 = leads from two days ago · PI = proforma invoice · DD = deal done · B1–B10 = day blocks (see page 1).
