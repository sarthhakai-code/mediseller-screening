Standing rules — all day स्थायी नियम

  • Reply to every WhatsApp message within 10 minutes. हर व्हाट्सऐप संदेश का जवाब 10 मिनट के भीतर दें।
  • Check and update all WhatsApp accounts every 15 minutes. हर 15 मिनट में सभी व्हाट्सऐप अकाउंट जाँचें और अपडेट करें।
  • Update customer status within 5 minutes of every interaction. हर बातचीत के 5 मिनट के भीतर ग्राहक की स्थिति अपडेट करें।
  • Update the Lead Sheet after every call or message; no pending updates. हर कॉल/संदेश के बाद लीड शीट अपडेट करें — कुछ भी लंबित न रहे।
  • Phones: follow MS-POL-01 (personal phone use ₹1,000 penalty). फ़ोन नीति MS-POL-01 का पालन करें।

Priority order for replies उत्तर प्राथमिकता क्रम

On-Payment→High Value→Active→Interested→New Leads

Who does what कौन क्या करेगा

Tasks fall into four groups. Common tasks are done by every executive, whether India or Global sales. Each group has its own one-page daily checklist.

Common All staff · MS-SOP-02

Essential tasks, WhatsApp replies, follow-ups, lead sheet, calling campaigns, end-of-day close.

Domestic India sales · MS-SOP-03

IndiaMART buy leads, India B2B + retail follow-ups, ExportersIndia / TradeIndia, the IndiaMART calling block.

Global Global sales · MS-SOP-04

IndiaMART Global activation, WeChat Moments, global follow-ups, email activation, international apps, Aramex quotes.

Activation Activation team · MS-SOP-05

Keyword calls, platform and buy-lead activation, database calling, chat archiving.

T-1 = leads from yesterday · T-2 = leads from two days ago · PI = proforma invoice · DD = deal done · B1–B10 = day blocks (see page 1).
