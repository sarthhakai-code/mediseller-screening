Do these international tasks in addition to the Common Tasks (MS-SOP-02). Global follow-ups come first: USA, Canada, UK, EU, Australia, Middle East.

Daily checklist दैनिक जाँच सूची

  • G01 · B1
    Post on WeChat Moments for a global audience: oncology, HIV, hepatitis, chronic care, supplements.
  • G02 · B2
    IndiaMART Global activation: open all Global leads from the past 24 h, send activation messages with CTA, mark responders "Activated".
  • G03 · B4
    Global follow-ups first: USA, Canada, UK, EU, Australia, Middle East. On-Payment messages, Active customers, ad leads.
  • G04 · B7
    Email global activation: daily emails to new global enquiries (company intro + product range + CTA + WhatsApp link). Minimum 15–20 per executive.
  • G05 · B7
    International apps: Telegram, Line, Viber, Signal, WeChat, Botim. Minimum 5 messages per app; reconnect previous leads; share new offers, new stock and best products; record in the Lead Sheet.
  • G06 · B7
    Message 10 customers in Hong Kong about ED products.
  • G07 · As needed
    Quote shipping from the Aramex rate card (MS-PL-02).
Block times: B1–B10 are counted from your shift start. See the day-structure table in MS-SOP-01 for the clock times of each shift.
