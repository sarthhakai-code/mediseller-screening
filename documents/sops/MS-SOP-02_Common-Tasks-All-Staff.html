Daily checklist दैनिक जाँच सूची

  • C01 · B1
    Upload WhatsApp status/story on all business devices (2 products: best products, new arrivals, offers, top sellers).
  • C02 · B1
    Send the daily broadcast message.
  • C03 · B2
    Archive the previous day's completed chats on all WhatsApp accounts.
  • C04 · B2
    Label every active chat: On-Payment, Interested, Active, Deal Done, High Value, Repeat Buyer. No active customer stays unlabelled.
  • C05 · B2
    Save yesterday's deal-done customers to phonebook + CRM as Name – Country – Product; sync WhatsApp and CRM.
  • C06 · B2
    Review yesterday's chats for anything pending.
  • C07 · B3 · all day
    Respond to all WhatsApp messages and queries in priority order On-Payment → High Value → Active → Interested → New Leads.
  • C08 · B3
    Reply to all Chatwoot conversations.
  • C09 · B4
    Follow-ups: On-Payment (PI sent, payment reminder), Interested (requested details), Active (awaiting confirmation). Record every follow-up in the Lead Sheet.
  • C10 · All day
    Keep the Lead Sheet live: Customer Name, Country, Product, Lead Type, Stage (New / Interested / On-Payment / Closed), Remarks & Next Follow-up Date.
  • C11 · As needed
    Prepare quotations and tag the lead type.
  • C12 · As needed
    Share the Telegram group link with customers.

Standing rules स्थायी नियम

  • Reply to every WhatsApp message within 10 minutes. हर व्हाट्सऐप संदेश का जवाब 10 मिनट के भीतर दें।
  • Check and update all WhatsApp accounts every 15 minutes. हर 15 मिनट में सभी व्हाट्सऐप अकाउंट जाँचें और अपडेट करें।
  • Update customer status within 5 minutes of every interaction. हर बातचीत के 5 मिनट के भीतर ग्राहक की स्थिति अपडेट करें।
  • Update the Lead Sheet after every call or message; no pending updates. हर कॉल/संदेश के बाद लीड शीट अपडेट करें — कुछ भी लंबित न रहे।
  • Phones: follow MS-POL-01 (personal phone use ₹1,000 penalty). फ़ोन नीति MS-POL-01 का पालन करें।
