Daily activation targets. Use the keyword-call list (MS-REF-02) and record only Active + Interested leads in the Lead Sheet.

Daily checklist दैनिक जाँच सूची

  • A08 · B2 · B7
    Chats: archiving and other apps (Viber, Signal etc.).
  • A01 · B3 · B8
    Keyword calls on scheme products: 25 calls per product × 4 products a day (100 calls), from MS-REF-02.
  • A02 · B3
    Platform activation: TradeIndia, ExportersIndia and IndiaMART (T-2). Message on IndiaMART, then call.
  • A03 · B5
    Buy-lead activation: last week's and T-2 buy leads.
  • A04 · B8
    Database calling: 2022 · 2023 · 2024 deal-done customers.
  • A05 · B8
    Database calling: old lead sheets (Riya, Disha, Shivani, Sahil etc.).
  • A06 · B8
    Database calling: new data — doctors, B2B, pharmacies.
  • A07 · B8
    Database calling: T-2 TradeIndia / ExportersIndia leads; RTO, cancelled and notebook leads.
  • A09 · All day
    Lead sheets updated properly. Record Active + Interested only.
