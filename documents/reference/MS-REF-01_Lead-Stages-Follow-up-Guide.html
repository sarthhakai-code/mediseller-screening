Stage & follow-up matrix चरण व फ़ॉलो-अप तालिका

Follow-up 1 is the first priority and Follow-up 4 the last. Red-stage leads get no follow-up.

Green stageQuotation / order details sharedFollow-up 2

Reply priority उत्तर प्राथमिकता

On-Payment→High Value→Active→Interested→New Leads

Follow-up categories श्रेणियाँ

  • On-Payment — received PI, needs a payment reminder
  • Interested — requested details
  • Active — ongoing conversation, awaiting confirmation

WhatsApp labels व्हाट्सऐप लेबल

On-Payment Interested Active Deal Done High Value Repeat Buyer

No active customer stays unlabelled.

Lead Sheet columns लीड शीट कॉलम

  • Customer Name · Country · Product · Lead Type
  • Stage: New / Interested / On-Payment / Closed
  • Remarks & Next Follow-up Date

Contact naming संपर्क नाम प्रारूप

Name – Country – Product

Save to phonebook + CRM, then sync with WhatsApp.
